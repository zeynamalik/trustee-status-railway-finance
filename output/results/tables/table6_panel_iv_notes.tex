Senior railway security-years, 1894--1913. Dependent variable: yield relative to the market median (bp), except column (4), where it is slot-measured eligibility. Columns (1)--(3) use within-security variation as companies gain or lose eligibility; dividend controls are the previous year's ordinary dividend and the ten-year mean. Columns (4)--(5) are a fuzzy RD with the running variable rebuilt from the dividend implied by the IMM's printed yield and price ($|X^{yld}| \le 1$), instrumenting slot-measured eligibility with yield-implied eligibility. Column (6) uses the same instrument in the fixed-effects model. Within-security estimates are identified only from the few companies whose eligibility changes, so their clustered standard errors are unreliable.
