Dependent variable: senior security yield relative to the market median, in basis points. Sample: 1894--1913. Column (1): local linear, triangular kernel, MSE-optimal bandwidth, year and security-class covariates; standard error, $p$-value and interval are robust bias-corrected (Calonico, Cattaneo and Titiunik 2014). Columns (2)--(6): local linear regression with separate slopes, uniform kernel, year and class fixed effects; standard errors clustered by company; $p$-values from a restricted wild-cluster bootstrap with Webb weights (999 replications). Column (5) drops company-years with a ten-year minimum of exactly 3.00 ($0 \le X < 0.125$). Column (6) averages each company's senior securities, weighted by nominal amount, and has year fixed effects only.
