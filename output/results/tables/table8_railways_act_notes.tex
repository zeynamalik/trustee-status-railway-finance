Monthly senior-security yields relative to the market median (bp), January 1919 to December 1922, for British companies amalgamated under the Railways Act 1921. Treated: the company had not paid 3\% in each of the ten years to 1920, so its senior stocks gained trustee status only through s.15 of the Act. All columns include security and calendar-month fixed effects (column (3): pair-specific). Column (3) matches each treated security to the control security of the same class with the closest 1919 mean yield. Standard errors clustered by company.
