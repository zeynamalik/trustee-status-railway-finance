Company-years with an ordinary dividend within 0.5 points of threshold $k$, in 1879--1887 and 1894--1913. Dependent variable: 1[dividend $\ge k$]. At stake: the company paid at least $k$\% in each of the previous nine years, so paying less now breaks a ten-year record. Only $k = 3$ has legal meaning (Trustee Act 1893). Standard errors clustered by company. $^{*}p<0.10$, $^{**}p<0.05$, $^{***}p<0.01$.
